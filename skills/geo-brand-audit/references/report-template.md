# GEO audit report template

## Scope and limitations

Brand, market, language, engines/models, collection dates, sample size, and known constraints.

## Observed visibility

Mention rate, citation rate, query-intent matrix, competitor observations, recurring sources, and answer-fit notes.

## Discoverability findings

Technical and content evidence, with affected URLs and evidence IDs.

## Priority gaps

For each gap: evidence IDs, observed fact, interpretation, confidence, and proposed test.

## Handoff

Inputs for `$geo-brand-optimizer`, plus baseline dataset location and comparable retest conditions.
